Frequently Asked Questions

We sort our computers into three categories:
  • Grade A: Best cosmetic and functional condition.
  • Grade B: Fully functional, but with more noticeable cosmetic blemishes (“scratch & dent”).
  • Parts: Not fully functional, used for spare components.

Will I receive the exact computer shown on your website?
Yes! For each listing, we photograph the actual unit you’ll receive, or in most cases, you'll get a better looking one. What you see is exactly what you get.

How will I track my shipment?
As soon as FedEx collects your package, you’ll receive a tracking number by email so you can monitor your computer’s journey to your doorstep.

Do you offer international shipping?
Yes, we offer international shipping to many countries. If you’re located outside the United States, you can place your order online as usual. Shipping rates and delivery times will be calculated at checkout based on your location and the shipping method selected. Please note that international customers are responsible for any customs duties, taxes, or import fees required by their country. If you have questions about shipping to your specific location or need a custom quote, feel free to contact our team before ordering—we’re happy to help you with the details.
